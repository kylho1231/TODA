⚖️ Municipal Ordinance Overcharging Penalties

Under San Jose de Buenavista Municipal Ordinance No. 2022-18, charging fares beyond the official tariff matrix carries statutory penalties:

  • 1st Offense: ₱1,000 fine & mandatory driver counseling
  • 2nd Offense: ₱1,500 fine & 30-day suspension of TODA franchise
  • 3rd Offense: ₱2,500 fine & permanent cancellation of MTOP franchise
